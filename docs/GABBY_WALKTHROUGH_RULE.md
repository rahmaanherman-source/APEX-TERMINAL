# Gabby Walkthrough Rule: take away the guessing

**Owner:** Mac (Rahmann Herman), 2026-10-10.
**Applies to:** Gabby, and every AI or person who guides someone through a screen in any APEX product.

## Who we are talking to

Our biggest audience is people who have **never used a computer**, or who grew up without the internet in their neighborhood. Treat every walkthrough as if it's their first time on a screen. If an expert reads it, they lose nothing. If a beginner reads it, they finish.

**The goal: the person never has to guess.**

## The rules

1. **Say where they are first.** Name the page they should be looking at and what's on it. Example: "You're on the Netlify page that says *Let's deploy your project with…*"
2. **One action per step.** Never put two clicks in one step.
3. **Say exactly where the thing is on the screen.** Top, bottom, left or right; what it's next to; whether they need to scroll. Example: "Scroll down a little. Under *Project name* there's a box labeled *Branch to deploy*."
4. **Use the exact words on the button, in bold.** Write **Deploy**, never "the blue button" alone. If the label isn't certain, say what to look for.
5. **Say what to type, exactly.** Example: type **apex-360-booth**.
6. **Say what to skip.** If a box or section should stay empty, say so out loud: "Leave the *environment variables* box empty and skip past it."
7. **Say what they'll see after.** "It will say *Building*, then *Published*." That way they know it worked.
8. **Give the direct link** whenever one exists, so they land on the exact page instead of hunting for it.
9. **Warn before the wrong turn.** If there's an easy mistake on that screen (a wrong repository, a credit-spending box), name it before they reach it.
10. **Check in.** End with one small ask: "Send me a picture of what you see now." Then give the next single step.
11. **Plain words.** No jargon without a one-line meaning. Use the full product name ("Google AI Studio", not "AI Studio").
12. **Never "just go to the page and do it."** That's guessing, and guessing is the failure.

## Template

```
You're on: <page name / what's at the top>
Step 1: <one action> — it's <where on the screen>. The button says **<exact words>**.
Type: **<exact text>** (if any)
Skip: <anything to leave alone>
You'll see: <what changes>
Send me a picture of what you see now.
```

## Why

Every guess is a place someone quits. APEX grows by reaching people other platforms leave behind, so the walkthrough is part of the product, not an extra.
